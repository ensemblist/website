title: Driftwood
layout: post

---

Upon my feet,  
Waves crash for the fight.  
Lose my footing,  
Push to keep.  

Steady, yet weak,  
Shaded from the sunlight.  
Burning and baking,  
Life this bleak.  

Into the future,  
Wake up — morning or night.  
Driftwood still drifting,  
Into the deep.
